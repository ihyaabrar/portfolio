# DESIGN.md: Ihya' Dev Pass

Direction for the portfolio of Ihya' Nashirudin Abrar. The owner approved every item marked **owner**; items marked **proposed** are Claude's choices, with the reason, and change if the owner says so.

## Identity (owner)

- The site is an event pass. The visitor meets a black ID card hanging from a lanyard, as if Ihya' were a speaker at an event named after the portfolio.
- Projects are tickets, research papers are talks, the career history is the programme, the contact block is the pass you take home.
- Hero: the physical lanyard card on the right, text on the left. One card design only (the default black card). No variant menu.
- Language: English only (the owner dropped Indonesian).
- Themes: light (warm paper) and dark (the reference's night studio: cool backdrop, ceiling spotlight on the card). A moon/sun icon switches them; the first visit follows the system setting.
- Status: open to freelance work, at a relaxed pace alongside research. A CV (PDF, generated by scripts/make_cv.py from real facts only) is linked from the hero and the contact pass.

## Palette (owner: light, black card, orange accent)

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#f1f1ee` | Page ground, like card stock |
| Ink | `#141413` | Text, the ID card, the contact pass |
| Muted | `#5f5f59` | Secondary text on paper (5.68:1) |
| Rule | `#d6d6ce` | Hairlines and perforations (non-text) |
| Accent on paper | `#b8420f` | The one accent for text on paper (4.85:1) |
| Accent on ink | `#ee6a1f` | The same accent on black surfaces (5.90:1) |

One accent, used at the key moments only: the second half of the hero headline, the "live" marker, the card number.

## Typography (proposed)

- **Archivo** (variable, width axis 62 to 125). Reason: event badges and posters print roles in condensed grotesk capitals ("SPEAKER"); one family gives the condensed poster voice for display and a normal width for reading.
- **IBM Plex Mono**, only for ticket data: IDs, dates, serial numbers. Reason: ticket stubs print their data in a fixed-width face. Never for headings, never with wide tracking.

## Identity motif (proposed)

The perforation: a dashed tear line with half-circle notches, taken from the ticket stub. It separates the stub on project tickets and the sections of the page.

## Dials (owner)

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

- Every section uses a different composition.
- Motion: the lanyard physics in the hero, a timeline rule that fills as the reader scrolls, hover states. Nothing loops except the lanyard's gentle sway, which stops under reduced motion.

## Content rules (owner)

- Real content only: GitHub (ihyaabrar), the LinkedIn export, published papers. No invented numbers or testimonials.
- The phone number from LinkedIn is not shown.
